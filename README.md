## Details

**Conrad Industries (CNRD)** - Jones Act-protected Gulf Coast shipbuilder with aging-fleet replacement and a Samsung HI LNG partnership driving multi-year backlog growth.
*Frameworks: three-scenario DCF, LFCF-to-equity, WACC (equity-only), sensitivity tables, trading comps (P/E, EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT), Porter's Five Forces, three-statement model, football-field.*

**US HY Corporate Credit Spread** - Short a basket of US tech high-yield bonds, hedged with 30Y Treasuries, betting credit spreads widen from historically tight levels.
*Frameworks: half-Kelly sizing, Poisson take-profit model, DV01/duration-neutral hedging, modified duration & convexity, OAS/I-spread screening, bond DCF, regression vs. VIX/SPX, scenario analysis.*

**Thales – Defending Tomorrow** - Thales should reject a dilutive Rheinmetall merger and instead acquire Almato to build a sovereign European defense-data platform.
*Frameworks: trading comps (EV/Rev, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT), precedent transactions, DCF (perpetuity + exit-multiple), sensitivity tables, WACC build-up, synergy estimation, merger-of-equals EPS accretion/dilution, three-scenario DCF, football-field.*
